How a systemcomes together.

AVION, block by block. Scroll to follow a mission from the camera to the drop.

  1. 01 · Perception

    See the drop zone

    A RealSense depth camera and a thermal camera feed OpenCV on the Raspberry Pi, which finds colour-marked zones and people who need help.

  2. 02 · Flight control

    Hold the aircraft

    The Pixhawk runs ArduPilot's attitude loop from GPS and IMU, drives four ESCs at 400 Hz and opens the payload hook on command.

  3. 03 · Autonomy

    Fly the mission

    The Pi flies waypoints over MAVLink while telemetry and video stream to a browser ground station with a live 3D twin. An operator stays in command of every drop.
